## 2. GAZA — Hospital struck in Khan Younis; six killed

- Event: Israeli airstrike hits the al-Yasin surgical building of Nasser Hospital, Khan Younis, southern Gaza.
- Location: Khan Younis, Gaza.
- Event date: **~00:30, Thu 13 Nov 2026** (overnight Wed→Thu). Published 13 Nov.
- What happened: Two missiles struck the upper floors of the surgical building; one failed to explode. Six killed, including general surgeon Dr. Rizk Abu Shaira and a 13-year-old boy; 27–28 wounded. Four departments suspended (radiology, labs, surgical operations, inpatient surgical wards). Deadliest attack on Gaza's health system since the ceasefire took effect 10 Oct 2025.
- Claims/dispute: IDF + Shin Bet say the strike targeted Hamas Khan Younis Brigade commander Muhammad Abu Ismail and others allegedly operating from the hospital; hospital director says no warning was given; one report says the named commander was NOT among the dead. Present the strike and toll as fact; do NOT assert the militant-presence claim or the warning claim either way.
- Why it matters: A hospital hit during a ceasefire; loss of the only hospital still standing in Khan Younis (serves ~500,000 people); tests the durability of the Oct 2025 truce.
- Confidence: HIGH on strike + toll (AP, BBC, The Guardian, AFP agree: 6 dead incl. surgeon + boy). MEDIUM on the targeting rationale (contested).
